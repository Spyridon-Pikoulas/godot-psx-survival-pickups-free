# PSX Survival Pickups Free

**15 free PS1-style survival horror items, ready to pick up** in your game: an iron key, a red keycard, a folded note that unfolds, a diary, a map of the house, a first aid kit, a pill bottle, a green herb, a box of 9mm and a box of shotgun shells, AA batteries, a gold coin, and the save room's typewriter, ink ribbon and item box. pickup.gd takes them into the player's items by name, saves at the typewriter for an ink ribbon and opens the item box; inspect.gd holds one up to turn over and read. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per item with its colliders and an inventory icon each. Free for commercial use.

### What's inside

- **Keys and documents:** an iron skeleton key, a red keycard, a note that unfolds, a diary that
  opens, a map of the house that unfolds; each with its text to read.
- **Medicine and ammo:** a first aid kit, a pill bottle, a green herb; a box of 9mm and a box of
  12 gauge shells whose lids open on the rounds, giving
  [PSX Firearms](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-firearms)' own ammo.
- **The save room:** a typewriter that types the save, an ink ribbon tin, an item box that opens;
  AA batteries and a gold coin.
- **A pickup script** (pickup.gd): an item goes into the player's items by name, the typewriter
  saves for an ink ribbon, the item box opens and shuts, with signals for each.
- **An inspect view** (inspect.gd): the item held up over the dimmed game, turned with the mouse,
  its title and text with it; E plays its reveal.
- **15 inventory icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, named as
  [PSX Horror UI](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-ui)'s are.
- One 1024x1024 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours, real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per item: box colliders on every part, an AnimationPlayer where it moves,
pickup.gd on each, one lit material so your flashlight falls on it, and a gallery. Tested on
Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# items: the player's, an Array of item names
$Typewriter.use(items)    # takes an "ink_ribbon", saves
inspect.open($Diary)      # held up to read; E opens it
```
